Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 2026

1. Data We Collect

When you create an account, we collect your name and email address. When you log expenses, we store the amount, category, date, and any description you provide. We do not collect payment information or any data beyond what you explicitly enter.

2. How We Use Your Data

Your data is used solely to operate Spendly — to display your expense history, generate summaries, and provide filtering. We do not use your financial data for advertising, analytics products, or any purpose beyond delivering the service to you.

3. Data Storage

Your data is stored securely on our servers. Passwords are hashed and never stored in plain text. We take reasonable technical measures to protect your data from unauthorized access, but no system can guarantee absolute security.

You may request deletion of your account and all associated data at any time by contacting us.

4. Third Party Services

Spendly does not share your personal or financial data with third parties. We do not integrate with advertising networks, data brokers, or analytics platforms that have access to your expense records.

We use Google Fonts to load typefaces for the interface. This request is made directly from your browser and is subject to Google's own privacy policy.

5. Contact Us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or want to request deletion of your data, please reach out to us. We are committed to responding to privacy-related requests promptly.
